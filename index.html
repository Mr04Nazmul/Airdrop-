How it works

1) Connect wallet. 2) Sign a one-time ownership message. 3) Start the client. 4) Every heartbeat is authenticated by the backend. 5) The backend awards points and records uptime. Refreshing or changing the browser does not create fake points.

This is a participation client, not a full blockchain validator. It does not claim to mine cryptocurrency or create tokens.
